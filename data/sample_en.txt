Artificial intelligence is changing how we build software. Large language
models can write code, answer questions, and summarize long documents. But a
language model alone does not know your private data. Retrieval augmented
generation solves this problem. It first searches a knowledge base, then puts
the retrieved documents into the prompt. The model reads the prompt and answers
based on the retrieved context. This is why retrieval matters. A good retrieval
system finds the right documents, and a good prompt tells the model how to use
them. Most production systems combine retrieval with prompt engineering, and
the quality of the retrieval often decides the quality of the final answer.
